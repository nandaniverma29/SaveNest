About SaveNest

We help shoppers pay less, with codes that are checked before you see them.

Who we are

SaveNest is an independent coupon and deals website. We bring promo codes and offers from online stores into one place, and we check them regularly so you spend less time on codes that fail at checkout.

Our story

We started SaveNest after one too many checkout pages where a promo code simply would not work. Many coupon sites were full of expired offers, hidden conditions and pop-ups.

We set out to build something simpler: a trustworthy place where every code is labelled, tested where possible and removed as soon as it stops working.

Our mission

To make every discount easy to find, easy to understand and honest about its conditions, so that anyone can shop smarter without spending hours searching.

How we verify offers

  • Collect: we gather offers from stores, affiliate partners and our community.
  • Test: our team tries codes at checkout wherever it is possible.
  • Label: each offer shows its terms, expiry and when it was last checked.
  • Remove: expired or failing offers are taken down as soon as we find them.

What we stand for

  • Accuracy: real discounts with clear conditions, never inflated savings.
  • Transparency: we explain how we earn money from our links.
  • Independence: stores cannot buy a higher ranking or hide their terms.
  • Privacy: we collect as little personal data as possible.

How we make money

SaveNest is free to use. When you click a link and buy from a store, that store may pay us a small commission. This never changes the price you pay, and it does not decide which offers we list or how they are ordered.

Editorial standards

  • Every offer shows its terms.
  • The date of the last check is visible.
  • We do not use fake discounts or false urgency.
  • Broken codes are reviewed quickly.
  • Sponsored content is clearly marked.

Contact us

Found a code that does not work, or want to suggest an offer? Visit our contact page and we will get back to you.
