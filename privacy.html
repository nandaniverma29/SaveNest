Privacy Policy

How SaveNest collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated: October 1, 2026.

1. Overview

We respect your privacy and collect only what we need to run the site. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • You give us: name, email and message when you contact us or submit an offer.
  • Collected automatically: pages viewed, device type, browser, approximate location and referring page.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and requests.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site.
  • To understand which coupons are useful.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies for basic functions and for analytics. Our store partners may also set cookies when you click through, so they can credit us for the visit. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.

5. Affiliate partners

When you click a coupon link, the destination store or its affiliate network may receive information such as your click and purchase, under their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help run the site, and when the law requires it.

7. Data retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, but no online service is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or object to certain uses. Send a request through our contact page and choose "Privacy request".

9. Children

SaveNest is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
